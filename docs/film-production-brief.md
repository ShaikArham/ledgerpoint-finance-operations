## 1. The complete 90-second film script

# LedgerPoint: The €12 Mystery

**Runtime:** 90 seconds
**Audience:** DataPoint Ireland hiring team
**Purpose:** Independent application for Accounts & Payroll Analyst
**Narration:** Natural, confident English voice. Conversational rather than dramatic.

### Scene 1 | 00:00–00:07 | The Hook

**Visual:** Start on black. A single fictional bank transaction appears. Its matching status changes to amber.

**On-screen text:**

€12.00
UNMATCHED TRANSACTION

IT'S ONLY €12.
SO WHY DOESN'T IT MATCH?

**Voiceover:**

“It's only twelve euro. So why doesn't it match?”

**Sound:** Single keyboard click, quiet low-frequency pulse, then half a second of silence.

**Transition:** Match-cut from the €12 amount to the bank statement containing the transaction.

### Scene 2 | 00:07–00:20 | The Problem

**Visual:** Show three authentic-looking financial records: an invoice register, a payment ledger and a bank statement. Matching transactions connect through fine lines. The €12 remains unmatched.

**On-screen text:**

ONE TRANSACTION.
THREE RECORDS.
NO CLEAR ANSWER.

**Voiceover:**

“Sometimes the smallest discrepancy raises the biggest question. Can we explain every number, from the original invoice to the final bank transaction?”

**Sound:** Soft ticking rhythm and subtle paper or interface sounds.

**Transition:** The three records align into one clean financial grid.

### Scene 3 | 00:20–00:34 | DataPoint Ireland

**Visual:** The financial grid resolves into elegant typography introducing DataPoint Ireland. Display its publicly described service areas without suggesting DataPoint owns this film.

**On-screen text:**

DATAPOINT IRELAND

ACCOUNTING
PAYROLL
FINANCIAL REPORTING
BUSINESS ANALYTICS
PROCESS IMPROVEMENT

**Voiceover:**

“That's what drew me to DataPoint Ireland. Your work brings together accounting, payroll, reporting and business analytics to help businesses make more informed decisions.”

**Sound:** Tension softens. A restrained piano or electronic theme enters.

**Transition:** Service labels become the headings of a job-description document.

### Scene 4 | 00:34–00:48 | The Position

**Visual:** Show a carefully recreated excerpt of the public DataPoint vacancy. Highlight responsibilities one at a time.

**On-screen text:**

ACCOUNTS & PAYROLL ANALYST

INVOICE PROCESSING
BANK RECONCILIATION
MONTHLY REPORTING
PAYROLL SUPPORT

**Voiceover:**

“Then I read your Accounts and Payroll Analyst vacancy. Instead of simply listing my skills in another CV, I wanted to demonstrate how I approach the work.”

**Sound:** A single soft keyboard keystroke marks the decision to build.

**Transition:** The job responsibilities transform into the navigation of the LedgerPoint application.

### Scene 5 | 00:48–01:00 | The Reveal

**Visual:** Reveal the original LedgerPoint logo. Then open the actual finance dashboard using recorded application footage.

**On-screen text:**

SO I BUILT SOMETHING.

LEDGERPOINT

AN INDEPENDENT FINANCE OPERATIONS PROTOTYPE

**Voiceover:**

“So I built LedgerPoint, an independent finance operations prototype inspired by DataPoint's work and the responsibilities of this position.”

**Sound:** Music becomes warmer and more confident.

**Transition:** Match-cut from the logo into the live application interface.

### Scene 6 | 01:00–01:18 | The Proof

**Visual:** Show actual LedgerPoint screens in this order:

1. Executive financial overview.
2. Invoice register.
3. Bank reconciliation.
4. The fictional unmatched €12 debit.
5. The investigation recommendation.

**On-screen text:**

€1,000 NET REVENUE
€700 OPERATING PROFIT
€730 RECEIVABLES

€12 UNMATCHED

DETECT → INVESTIGATE → REVIEW

**Voiceover:**

“It brings invoices, reconciliation and financial reporting into one demonstration. Here, a twelve-euro bank debit has no ledger match. LedgerPoint flags it for investigation rather than inventing an explanation.”

**Sound:** Restrained interface clicks. Music briefly drops beneath the unmatched transaction reveal.

**Transition:** The €12 exception returns to the centre of the frame, resolving the opening mystery by showing its correct investigation status.

### Scene 7 | 01:18–01:30 | The Candidate and Invitation

**Visual:** Show the candidate's name, relevant qualifications and LedgerPoint logo. Finish on the actual application's entry screen.

**On-screen text:**

SHAIK ARHAM SAQUIB

MSc Computing (Data Analytics)
2.5+ Years · Business Intelligence

THIS IS MY APPLICATION.

EXPLORE LEDGERPOINT

**Voiceover:**

“I'm Shaik Arham Saquib. With experience in business intelligence and data analytics, this is how I chose to apply. Welcome to LedgerPoint.”

**Sound:** Music resolves naturally, followed by a soft interface confirmation.

**Transition:** The film fades into the interactive LedgerPoint application.

**Final disclaimer:** Independent fictional portfolio demonstration. Not commissioned or endorsed by DataPoint Ireland.

The voiceover should be recorded and timed against the edit. The target is exactly 90 seconds, including opening silence and final transition. Adjust pauses and shot holds before changing the wording.

## Visual production

Produce a genuine 4K video with:

- The exact on-screen text and narration in the supplied script.
- High-quality motion typography.
- Realistic, internally consistent financial records.
- Authentic LedgerPoint screen recordings.
- Professional narration and sound design.
- Original or licensed music.
- Smooth, intentional transitions.
- Correctly rendered € currency symbols and all financial values.

Do not use AI-generated people, synthetic offices, fake interviews, random stock footage, excessive particle effects, artificial lens flares or generic futuristic animations.

Use the existing LedgerPoint visual identity.

## Product authenticity

Inspect the actual application before recording.

The fictional financial scenario must remain consistent:

- €1,000 net revenue.
- €300 operating expenses.
- €700 operating profit.
- €730 outstanding receivables.
- €131 posted settlement cash movement.
- €12 unmatched bank debit, reference UNKNOWN-001.

Do not treat the unmatched €12 as an approved expense or as a confirmed fraud incident.

Do not fabricate interactive features or claim production-grade accounting, payroll, VAT, authentication or audit capabilities.

## Website integration

Update the existing website, preserving its application and navigation.

Add:

- Responsive cinematic video player.
- Play, pause, seek, mute and replay.
- Captions and transcript.
- Skip to Application.
- Enter LedgerPoint after playback.
- A replay option in the application.
- A lightweight poster image.
- Graceful fallback if video playback fails.
- Reduced-motion and keyboard accessibility.
- Session memory for completed or skipped introductions.

The video must not block access to the working application.
