# Phase 4.1: Financial statement engine

## Reports
- Trial balance with debit and credit columns
- Profit and loss with revenue, expenses and net profit
- Balance sheet with assets, liabilities, contributed equity and
  cumulative retained profit
- As-of date filtering, including reversal effects

## Example
A fictional company receives EUR 1,000 of owner capital, invoices EUR 500
of revenue and pays EUR 200 in operating expenses.
- Revenue EUR 500; expenses EUR 200; net profit EUR 300
- Assets EUR 1,300; liabilities EUR 0
- Contributed equity EUR 1,000; retained profit EUR 300
- Accounting equation: EUR 1,300 = EUR 0 + EUR 1,300

## Limitations
These are simplified cumulative reports for educational demonstration,
not statutory Irish financial statements. There is no period-specific
P&L, closing journal, comparative period, accrual schedule, depreciation,
inventory accounting, bank opening balance import, or retained-earnings
roll-forward across years. VAT handling remains illustrative. A balanced
report does not prove the underlying classification is correct.
